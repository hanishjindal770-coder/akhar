Matras and other signs are shown on a dotted circle ◌ to mark where the letter goes. For a colour-coded picture of the keyboard with fingers marked, open the interactive keyboard.

Combinations to remember

  • ਕਿ = K, F (sihari after the letter)
  • ਪ੍ਰ = H, D, J or H, Shift+3
  • ਪੜ੍ਹ = H, Shift+J, D, U or H, Shift+J, Shift+`
  • ਪੱਕਾ = H, Shift+4, K, E
  • ਜ਼ = P, ]
  • ਮਾਂ = C, E, Shift+X

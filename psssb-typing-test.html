      About the PSSSB typing test

      Posts such as Clerk, Clerk-cum-Data Entry Operator, Legal Clerk and similar posts filled by the Punjab Subordinate Services Selection Board usually have a qualifying typing test after the written exam. You type one passage in Punjabi and one in English on a computer. Marks are not added to your merit; you only need to qualify.

      • Punjabi: Unicode Raavi font on the INSCRIPT keyboard. See the layout chart and the Raavi typing guide.
      • English: a normal QWERTY keyboard.
      • Standard: 30 words per minute with at least 92% accuracy in each language.
      • Time: 10 minutes per passage in this mock. Check the time and rules in your own notice, because they can change between recruitments.

      This mock follows the format and scoring in published Punjab typing-test notices. It is an independent practice tool, not the official test.

      How this mock scores you

      • Full mistake: a wrong, missing or extra word. In Punjabi a wrong matra, addak or half letter makes the whole word wrong.
      • Half mistake: a spacing error, where two words are joined or one is split.
      • Error characters = mistakes × 5, and net characters = gross characters − error characters.
      • Gross speed = gross characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Accuracy = net characters ÷ gross characters × 100.

      The full method with a worked example is in Punjab typing test rules and tips. If you are still learning the keys, start with the 57 free lessons.

      Frequently asked questions

      What typing speed is needed for the PSSSB clerk test?

      Most PSSSB clerk-type recruitments ask for 30 words per minute in Punjabi and 30 words per minute in English, with at least 92% accuracy. Always confirm the figure in the notice for your post.

      Which font and keyboard are used for the Punjabi typing test?

      Punjabi is typed in the Unicode Raavi font on the INSCRIPT keyboard, the default Punjabi keyboard in Windows. This mock test uses the same layout.

      Is Backspace allowed in the PSSSB typing test?

      It depends on the notice. This mock lets you practise both ways: the standard tests allow Backspace and the strict mock switches it off.

      How is net speed calculated?

      Every full mistake costs 5 characters and every half mistake 2.5 characters. Net characters are gross characters minus these error characters, and net speed is net characters divided by 5 and by the minutes taken.

      Is this the official PSSSB test?

      No. This is a free, independent practice test that follows the format and scoring published in Punjab typing-test notices. It is not connected with PSSSB.

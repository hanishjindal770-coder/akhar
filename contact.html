Found a mistake in a lesson, a passage with a spelling error, or a bug in the exam scoring? We want to hear about it.

How to reach us

The quickest way is to open an issue on the project's GitHub page. You need a free GitHub account.

Send feedback on GitHub

Please include:

  • which page or lesson number you were on,
  • what you typed and what you expected to happen,
  • your device and browser (for example, Windows 11 with Chrome, or an Android phone).

Suggestions for passages

If you have a Punjabi passage from a past typing test or a topic you would like to practise, send it along. Passages are reviewed for spelling before they are added.

Exam questions

We cannot answer questions about a specific recruitment, admit cards or results. Please contact the board that published the notice.

How the typing test is scored

  • Punjabi is typed in Unicode Raavi font on the INSCRIPT keyboard. The test runs for 10 minutes.
  • To qualify you need at least 30 words per minute and at least 92% accuracy.
  • Full mistake: a missed word, a wrong word, an extra word, or missing punctuation. A wrong or missing matra, addak or half letter makes the whole word wrong.
  • Half mistake: a spacing error, such as two words joined or one word split.
  • Vowels are typed as single keys. ਉ is Shift+G, never ੳ followed by ੁ.
Formula used here error chars = mistakes × 5 net chars = gross chars − error chars gross WPM = gross chars ÷ 5 ÷ minutes net WPM = net chars ÷ 5 ÷ minutes accuracy = net chars ÷ gross chars × 100 Example: 1,650 characters with 6 full and 2 half mistakes in 10 minutes gives 7 mistakes, 35 error characters, gross 33 WPM, net 32.3 WPM and 97.9% accuracy. Always check the rules in your own recruitment notice.

Before you start

  • Type on your normal English keyboard. Akhar maps each key to the INSCRIPT layout, so you do not need to change Windows settings.
  • If the Windows Punjabi keyboard is already on, that works too.
  • Press Space once between words. Extra spaces are ignored, missing ones count as half mistakes.
  • Paste is blocked, just like the exam software.
  • Leaving a word half typed when time runs out counts as a mistake, so finish the word you are on.

Typing on your own computer

In Akhar you can type straight away: every key on an English keyboard is converted to the INSCRIPT character shown in the chart.

To type Punjabi in other programs, as in the exam hall, add the Punjabi language in Windows Settings under Time & language. Its default keyboard is this same INSCRIPT layout, and Raavi font comes with Windows. Switch with Win + Space.

Akhar shows text in Raavi when your computer has it, and in Noto Sans Gurmukhi on phones and other systems. The characters are the same Unicode in both.
